# Sustainability Quest — EN

Collaborative board game for applied sustainability learning: **48 situational questions**, eight in each of six categories.

## Learning topics
1. Eco-Design & Lifecycle.
2. Operations & Energy.
3. Circularity & Materials.
4. Supply Chain & Compliance.
5. Evidence & Governance.
6. Risk & Resilience.

## Gameplay
One to four players roll a die, choose routes, answer questions with explanations, earn six topic badges and complete a final challenge. The original animated board, scoring and game rules are preserved while the curriculum has been replaced to broaden its application.

**Saved-game note:** since all six badge meanings have changed, this release uses a separate v3 saved-game key. Old games are not migrated or automatically removed from the browser.

## Training scope
These are fictional scenarios for practising evidence-based choices, not Airbus-approved procedures, engineering release authority, certifications or verified operational performance.
