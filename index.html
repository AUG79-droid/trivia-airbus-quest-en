How this version works

Classic turn logic

  • You roll first.
  • Then you choose a legal destination reachable with the exact die value.
  • You only get a question after the movement is completed.
  • Correct answers keep the turn alive. Wrong answers pass the turn.
  • Once all six wedges are collected, the team must return to the centre with the exact number.
