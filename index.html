Rules

How to play Trivia Airbus Quest

  1. Two teams alternate turns.
  2. Each turn starts by rolling a 1–6 die.
  3. The landing colour determines the question category.
  4. A correct answer unlocks that wedge for the team.
  5. Wedges are not duplicated: once collected, that colour stays active.
  6. The first team to complete all five wedges wins immediately.
